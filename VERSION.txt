makeaibilli v3.0 (final)
========================
Trading intelligence dashboard — stocks + crypto — 24/7 signal collection,
factor-based ranking, forward projections, pre-market decision passes.

Highlights:
  · Per-asset SQLite databases (one .db per stock/coin, 30-day retention)
  · 6-factor composite score (momentum, trend, mean-reversion, volume,
    relative strength, intel) z-scored across the universe
  · Unique-tier assignment: every asset appears in exactly one tab
  · Forward-looking expected-move projection (range + confidence bands)
  · Pre-market decision passes at 08:40 and 09:25 ET
  · Asset enrichment: Wikipedia + Wikidata for stocks, CoinGecko for crypto
    (description, founders, founding date, headquarters — cached 30 days)
  · IPO calendar with rich enrichment; Newly Listed tab for IPOs now trading
  · Coinbase new-listing auto-detection (dynamic crypto universe)
  · Full-screen chart page + "Open in TradingView ↗" escape hatch
  · Position calculator on every asset (quantity × price = total)
